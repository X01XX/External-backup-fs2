Added structs for mask and state.  The structs contain the number of bits they use.

A pn=1 square should be considered changed at the second sample, since it will be incompatible to a pn=2 square.
 
Improved arg checking. It can now be turned off, see globals.fs.

Allow union of rules ( 0->0, 0->1 ) ( 1->1, 1->0 ), to make ( XX, Xx) or ( X0, X1 ).

Separate adjacent-incompatible pairs, and possible regions, from non-adjacent-incompatible pairs, and possible regions.
Non-adjacent pairs must be within at least one region calculated from only adjacent-incompatible pairs.
